Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our analytics studio.

What types of data can FJ Watches Analytics Studio integrate with?

Our studio integrates with major e-commerce platforms, ERP systems, and point-of-sale software specifically tailored for jewelry and watch retailers to centralize your operations.

Can I customize the automated performance metrics?

Yes, all dashboards and performance metrics can be fully customized. You can define your own KPIs, track specific collections, and adjust reporting intervals to suit your business needs.

How secure is the user access control system?

We provide enterprise-grade security with role-based access control (RBAC). You can strictly manage permissions so that sensitive data is only accessible to authorized personnel.

Does the platform support multi-store locations?

Absolutely. You can monitor inventory movement, sales data, and staff performance across multiple store locations and compare them in real-time within a single dashboard.

How long does it take to set up the workspace?

Typical onboarding takes between 1 to 2 weeks depending on the complexity of your existing data structures. Our team provides full support during the integration process.
